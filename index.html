English phonology for the agrégation

Study notes for the phonology part of the épreuve de linguistique: the rules you need to justify a transcription, a stress pattern, a vowel value, a connected-speech process or an intonation pattern. Each chapter can be read on its own; the practice pages give you texts to work on.

How to use this site

  1. Read the chapter announced for the week before class. The left menu (or the Menu button on a phone) opens every chapter with its sections.
  2. Learn the rules as sentences you can write down in English. The exam rewards a rule stated in full, then applied.
  3. In the Graphophonemics chapter, switch the Transcriptions display to quiz: transcriptions are blurred until you tap a word. Test yourself before you check.
  4. Use the practice texts to write full answers under time, then compare with the chapter.
